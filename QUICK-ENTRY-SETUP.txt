MONTHLY QUICK ENTRY + DAILY ARROW NAVIGATION

Upload ALL files in this folder to the existing GitHub project.
Refresh the app after upload (Ctrl+Shift+R on the laptop).

Desktop: hover over a feeder to enter selected-date closing meter readings.
Click the feeder to open its saved monthly register.
Phone: first tap opens quick entry; second tap opens the register.
The popup contains Import and Export for both ends (HV/LV for ICTs).
Date comes from the shared Energy report date.
Values auto-save on leaving a reading box or pressing Enter.
If saving fails, readings remain in the popup and leaving is blocked until saved.
Calculations, differences, losses and exports stay in the complete register.
All 10 feeder/ICT buttons support quick entry.
Daily Consumption numeric spinners are hidden. Arrow keys move between editable boxes; calculated fields are skipped.
Includes the previous common-date linking update.
