COMMON REPORT BUTTONS AND INTERRUPTION PERIODS

This update adds:
1. Interruption periods: all saved dates, calendar month, rolling 1/3/6 months, 1 year and custom From/To.
2. Equipment history applies the chosen period to one equipment; Period-wise — all equipment includes every equipment in 400 kV, 220 kV, ICT order.
3. Detail rows, equipment totals and overall sum of confirmed durations. Unconfirmed timings are listed and excluded from duration totals.
4. A Back link plus direct 400 KV MD and 220 KV MD shortcuts on the interruption page.
5. A common bottom action bar: View, Print, PDF, Excel, Word, WhatsApp with vector symbols and consistent colours.

APPLIES TO
Home MD reports, Daily Consumption, Energy Meter Readings, Monthly Energy selector / register, Consolidated Reports, Interruptions.
Login and index redirect are not report pages and were left alone.

EXPORT DETAILS
Existing daily / energy / consolidated Print, Excel, Word and WhatsApp handlers are reused, preserving their established formats and save guards.
View previews saved daily text; other data-entry previews show currently displayed readings and identify that unsaved edits may be present.
Consolidated View uses the original View Report action to load its selected date.
New Excel exports for interruptions, demand and monthly registers use real .xlsx via ExcelJS. Internet is needed to load the export library on first use.
New Word exports use Word-compatible HTML .doc, matching the existing project's Word approach. They are not .docx.
PDF opens the browser print dialog: choose Save as PDF. It does not silently download a PDF.
WhatsApp opens a share composer; it does not send automatically. Long new reports are copied to clipboard and WhatsApp opened for pasting, or a message asks you to share Word/Excel if clipboard access is unavailable.

PERIOD DEFINITIONS
Rolling periods end on the selected To date. For example Last 3 months ending 13/10/2026 includes 14/07/2026 through 13/10/2026.
Calendar month selects the complete month. Custom dates include both endpoints.
Entries are grouped by start date and include their full outage duration. They are not clipped at date-range or month boundaries.
Overall duration adds per-equipment durations, not unique station downtime. Simultaneous interruptions on different equipment count separately.
Blank timings never become zero-duration confirmed records.

INSTALL
Back up your current folder first. Extract the updated ZIP in a separate folder.
Copy all updated HTML pages and new/updated feature files into your current project folder, or open the complete extracted project for testing.
Hard refresh Ctrl+Shift+R after opening with Live Server.
No additional Firebase rules are needed beyond the Demand/Interruptions rules already supplied.
