HOME — MAXIMUM DEMAND SO FAR

Home contains MD SO FAR with 400 KV MD and 220 KV MD side submenus. Select a submenu to open that group. Only maximum demand is displayed; minimum demand was removed at your request.
Enter MW and record date. Time is optional. Save each equipment separately. Values are saved to demandRecords with version checks to avoid overwriting another device's change.
The existing Demand Firebase rules are sufficient. Viewers read only; enabled admin/editor users can edit.
The INTERRUPTIONS tab is below MD SO FAR. Both tabs follow the top Home width/left alignment with compact height.
Auto comparison with daily readings and LC filtering remain deferred. Current demand records are manual only.
